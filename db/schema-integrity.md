# Structural schema integrity

[baseline.sql](baseline.sql) owns storage integrity for every writer. It is applied only to a fresh
installation; the administration tool checks its identity before use. Domain task declarations own
admission, permissions, roles, state transitions and business limits. The current durable grammar
belongs to [RecordFormat](../src/ClaimCore.RecordFormat), with permanent scalar representations in
[Domain values](../src/ClaimCore.Domain/Values.fs). Native decoding and verification retain
responsibility for semantic and per-field agreement; SQL guards do not reproduce task policy.
The [ledger structure checks](../tests/ClaimCore.IntegrationTests/Storage/LedgerStructureTests.fs)
exercise the current installation format with a fresh database and require the installation row,
ledger row and append operation to reject an incompatible format.

The following inventory covers the structural boundaries affected by the current baseline. SQL names
identify their implementation, rather than making this document a second source for expressions or
limits. All tests use synthetic data.

Permanent representability checks deliberately admit values that current business admission might
refuse, including a retained negative amount. Those checks protect faithful decoding of accepted
history rather than silently imposing today's policy on old records. Operational session payload and
calendar framing are separate from ledger business text admission.

Recovery publications and key rewrap serialize on the ledger head before changing the publication
generation. Prepared evidence also binds the exact maintenance handle and expiry frontier.
The owner-only attachment payload repair preserves the accepting identity and requires the
original reference and retained compartment. The structural repair path refuses erased owners.
Native replay and byte verification establish the retained descriptor and content before repair.

Digest claims are unique per recipient and business day and preserve their attempt identity,
deadline and first start. The database guard rejects updates to terminal outcomes. Core supplies
the outcome vocabulary and decides when an expired incomplete attempt becomes unknown.

An accepted erasure retains the family sequence and a digest of the exact submitted review token. Replay validates the family sequence and erasure coverage. Once protected dependencies are cleared, replay cannot recover the relationship set that was compared at admission.
